About the show

Signal & Noise is long-form conversation with people worth listening to — founders, clinicians, creators and operators. The premise is simple: most of what surrounds a subject is noise, and the useful part is the signal. Each episode goes looking for it.

Hosted by Nnamdi Okorafor — RN, clinical nurse manager, and builder of AI systems for healthcare and small business. Video first, because that is where the audience actually is.
